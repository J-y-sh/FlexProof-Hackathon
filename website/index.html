01 • Problem Analysis

Why Reactive Grid Control Fails at the Edge

As rooftop solar PV, electric vehicles, and heavy cooling loads expand on residential feeders, traditional threshold control triggers severe unintended battery degradation and residual deficits.

STEP 01
Renewable Intermittency
Rooftop solar output ramps down rapidly in the late afternoon just as domestic cooking, cooling, and commuter EV charging ramp up.
STEP 02
Forecast Uncertainty
Without lookahead forecasting, the distribution substation has zero visibility into steep coincidental evening net demand ramps.
STEP 03
Feeder Capacity Stress
Net load exceeds the 2,300 kW firm feeder rating, creating severe thermal stress and risking transformer asset degradation.
STEP 04
Reactive Actuator Delay
Threshold control acts only after limits are breached. Municipal water pumps and agricultural wells require 15–30 min to spool down and deliver zero immediate relief.
STEP 05
Battery Wear & Deficit
Batteries are forced into emergency high-rate discharge, accumulating 1,552.60 kWh of cycling throughput while still leaving unserved energy deficits.
02 • Control Architecture

The Five-Stage Predictive Operating Loop

FlexProof executes a continuous closed-loop sequence across every 15-minute dispatch interval to prepare flexible assets before physical limits are breached.

STAGE 01
Predict
Forecast renewable intermittency and net demand 1 hour ahead (4 intervals) from rolling telemetry without future data leakage.
STAGE 02
Prepare
Pre-shift flexible demand resources (water pumping, agricultural wells, EV charging) with 15–30 min lead time before the stress window.
STAGE 03
Coordinate
Coordinate flexible loads, battery storage envelope (50%–90% SOC), and dynamic reserve margins based on forecast gap severity.
STAGE 04
Dispatch
Deploy available load flexibility during the critical interval, using the community battery as a fine-tuning precision buffer.
STAGE 05
Learn
Verify response using measurable feeder outcomes; track error residuals to refine lookahead confidence bounds continuously.
03 • Flexibility Resources

Three Coordinated Resources

Rather than dumping battery energy reactively, FlexProof coordinates a multi-tier flexibility portfolio across the neighbourhood.

DEMAND SIDE

Flexible Loads (362 kW Pool)

Distributed multi-fleet demand management accounting for physical response latencies and process constraints. 100% of deferred energy is restored during off-peak valley hours (00:00–05:30).

  • EV Smart Charging 140 kW flex • 15 min delay
  • Municipal Water Pumping 105 kW flex • 30 min delay
  • Agricultural Irrigation 60 kW flex • 30 min delay
  • Commercial HVAC 45 kW flex • 15 min delay
  • Commercial Refrigeration 12 kW flex • 15 min delay
STORAGE SIDE

Community Battery (BESS)

500 kWh community energy storage system reserved strictly for precision buffering of residual forecast error, eliminating unnecessary full-power cycling bursts.

  • Nominal Capacity 500 kWh
  • Max Inverter Power 150 kW discharge
  • Safe Operational SOC 50.0% – 90.0%
  • Round-Trip Efficiency 95% continuous
  • Throughput Reduction ↓ 53.50% vs Reactive
RELIABILITY SIDE

Dynamic Reserve Capacity

Pre-allocated headroom maintained in the battery to absorb cloud-induced solar ramp events without triggering feeder overloads or curtailment.

  • Feeder Firm Rating 2,300 kW Limit
  • Solar Fleet Capacity 800 kW Rooftop PV
  • Intermittency Events 24 solar events modeled
  • Max Solar Ramp-Down ~285.5 kW observed
  • Reserve Actions Logged 148 reserve allocations

06 • System Architecture

Telemetry & Coordination Pipeline

Structured dataflow from historical telemetry ingestion to closed-loop dispatch verification.

01

Solar & Demand Signals Ingestion

24-hour rolling historical telemetry buffer (96 intervals) capturing diurnal demand curves and solar generation.

↓
02

1-Hour Ahead Forecasting Engine

Multi-lag statistical regression projecting net demand across the upcoming 4 intervals without future data leakage.

↓
03

Predictive Flexibility Coordinator

Evaluates fleet response latencies (15–30 min) and schedules advance pre-dispatch of water pumps, agricultural wells, and EVs.

↓
04

Real-Time Feeder Balancing & Dispatch

Applies active load adjustments while coordinating community BESS as a smooth precision buffer within 50%–90% SOC bounds.

↓
05

Invariant Verification & Control Metrics

Continuous validation of feeder limits, energy conservation, forecast residuals, and battery health logging.
